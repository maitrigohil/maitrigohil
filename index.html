Maitri Gohil

Hi, I'm Maitri, a Computer Science graduate. I'm interested in the organizational and individual-level implications of AI adoption: how firms restructure roles and workflows around generative AI, how employees adapt (or resist) these shifts, and what new forms of human-AI collaboration emerge as a result.

I currently work as a Research Assistant at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad (IIMA), and previously spent a summer as a research intern at the Indian Institute of Management Indore (IIMI). My research has been presented at the India Strategy Conference (ISC) and the IMC-IRC Conference at IIFT Delhi, with an abstract accepted for the INFORMS Annual Meeting in Atlanta, 2025. I was awarded the AOM STR Fellowship at the ISC conference by the Strategic Management (STR) Division of the Academy of Management (AOM).
